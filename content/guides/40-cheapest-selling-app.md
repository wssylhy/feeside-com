## The three things the ranking hides

Vinted winning every row does not make it the answer, because fees buy different things. Vinted's zero comes from charging the buyer instead, and its marketplace is fashion-only. Depop's second place is the payment line plus nothing, with a streetwear-heavy audience. Mercari and Etsy trade places near a $50 sale total: Etsy's 9.5% stack beats Mercari's flat 10% once the sale is big enough. eBay sits below them in cash terms but brings the largest buyer base, and Poshmark's bottom position is real money on expensive items, $30 on a $110 sale, for an audience that pays fashion prices.

## FAQ

**Which selling app has the lowest fees overall?**
Vinted, at zero seller-side fees on every price we checked. Depop is the closest fee-charging platform, taking only its payment line.

**Is the cheapest app always the best choice?**
No. The cheapest platform that has a buyer for your item beats the cheapest platform that does not. Fees decide it between platforms that both work for your category.

**Where does the Mercari and Etsy order flip?**
Around a $50 sale total. Below it Mercari's flat 10% costs less; above it Etsy's 9.5% plus fixed processing edges ahead, before Etsy's listing fee.
