## How the Etsy side works
Listing fee per four-month period (charged even without a sale), transaction fee on the total including shipping, processing on top — and a possible Offsite Ads line when an Etsy-placed ad converts (its own calculator, linked below). The stack buys search-and-craft buyer intent no other platform replicates.

## How the Poshmark side works
One commission line at $15+, one flat fee under it. No listing fees, no processing line, buyer-paid standard shipping outside your fee base. The trade is a social, closet-culture audience rather than Etsy's search-driven craft buyers.

## Worked example (example only)
A $45 handmade-style bag: Etsy's three lines vs Poshmark's one. The nets print side by side — and the per-item, per-month context matters: Etsy's listing fee recurs every four months whether or not the item sells, which the calculators note in the line labels.

## Which fits your shop
- **Handmade and craft buyers:** Etsy's audience intent usually justifies its stack — the fee buys demand that Poshmark's feed does not carry.
- **Vintage clothing in volume:** Poshmark's single line and closet economics usually win the math in the mid-price band.
- **Either way:** compare at your real price — the gap flips by band and by how long items sit (Etsy's recurring listing fee punishes slow inventory).

## FAQ
**Sell handmade on Etsy or Poshmark?**
True handmade: Etsy's buyer intent is the deciding advantage despite the fee stack. Vintage/thrift in volume: run both calculators — Poshmark's one-line math usually nets more in the mid band.

**Does Poshmark charge like Etsy's listing fee?**
No — no per-listing fee on Poshmark; its only line is the commission (or flat fee under $15).
