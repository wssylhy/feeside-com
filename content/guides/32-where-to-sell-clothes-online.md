## Matching the platform to the item
Everyday brand clothing and casual closet cleanouts lean toward Vinted's zero-commission line or Depop's processing-only line, which keep the most on paper.
Streetwear and vintage with photo-driven buyers do well on Depop, whose audience browses like an app, not a search engine.
Mid-price women's fashion with an engaged buyer base fits Poshmark, accepting its {{rates.poshmark.final_value_percent}}% as the price of that audience.
Collectibles, general merchandise, and anything that needs auction format or deep search go to eBay, where the stack buys reach.
Handmade goods have a dedicated home on Etsy, whose triple fee line funds exactly that niche.
