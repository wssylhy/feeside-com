## Matching the platform to the clothes

- Everyday secondhand clothing: Vinted. It takes nothing from the seller, and the buyer pays shipping.
- Streetwear and vintage: Depop for a style-driven audience that browses a feed. eBay for pieces that buyers search for by name.
- Fashion sold through offers and bundles: Poshmark. Its {{rates.poshmark.final_value_percent}}% is the highest fee of the six, paid for a social selling format.
- Handmade clothing and vintage that is 20 years or older: Etsy, which also sells craft supplies.
- Clothing that fits none of the above: Mercari or eBay, which list almost every category.

## FAQ

**What is the best app to sell clothes on?**
By fees, Vinted. It leaves the most at every price in the table. For streetwear, Depop has the next lowest fee. For a piece that buyers hunt for by name, eBay's wider reach can matter more than its fee.

**Which platform pays the most for used clothes?**
Vinted leaves the most at every price, because it charges sellers nothing on standard sales. Depop is second.

**Where can I sell clothes online for free?**
Vinted. Listing is free and there is no commission. The buyer pays Vinted a protection fee at checkout.

**Is Poshmark or Vinted better for selling clothes?**
By fees, Vinted at every price. Poshmark takes {{rates.poshmark.final_value_percent}}% of the item price on sales of ${{rates.poshmark.commission_threshold_price}} and up.

**Does Depop or Vinted pay more?**
