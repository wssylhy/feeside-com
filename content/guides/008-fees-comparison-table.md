# Resale Marketplace Fees Comparison 2026: All Six Platforms on One Page

Intro: eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted, Depop and Etsy each advertise a headline rate. What lands in your payout depends on what that rate is applied to and on the fixed fees around it. Every number on this page is calculated from the same fee data as the calculators on this site, and each calculator shows the date its rates were last checked.

## What each row means

- Selling fee: the platform's main percentage. "Of the sale total" means the shipping the buyer pays is inside the fee.
- Fixed fee per order: a flat amount on every order, whatever the price. It weighs heaviest on cheap items.
- Payment processing: a separate line on Depop and Etsy. The other platforms fold it into the selling fee or do not charge it.
- Listing fee: charged when you list, whether or not the item sells. eBay gives every seller a monthly allowance of free listings first.

## FAQ

**Which selling app has the lowest fees?**
Vinted. It charges sellers no commission, no per-order fee and no listing fee on standard sales, and charges the buyer a protection fee at checkout instead. Of the five that charge sellers, Depop is next lowest at every price in the table, then Etsy and Mercari, then eBay. Poshmark charges the most from $15 up.

**Which marketplace has the highest fees?**
From $15 up, Poshmark, which takes {{rates.poshmark.final_value_percent}}% of the item price. Under $15 its flat {{rates.poshmark.min_commission}} takes the largest share of any platform in the table above.

**Is Vinted really free for sellers?**
Yes, on standard sales. Sellers pay no commission and no listing fee. The buyer pays Vinted a protection fee at checkout.

**Do marketplace fees change?**
Yes. Mercari's {{rates.mercari.final_value_percent}}% selling fee, for example, applies to listings created or updated since January 6, 2025. Each calculator on this site shows the date its rates were last checked.

**Are buyer protection fees my cost?**
No. Vinted charges its protection fee to the buyer at checkout. It raises the buyer's total, which can affect what they will pay for your item, and it does not reduce your payout.
