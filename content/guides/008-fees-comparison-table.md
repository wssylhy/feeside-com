# Marketplace Fees Comparison 2026: All Six Platforms on One Page

Intro: Every selling app advertises a headline rate; the payout you actually receive stacks two or three lines on top of it. This comparison lays out all six marketplaces — eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted, Depop, Etsy — with each fee line separated, so the difference between "the advertised rate" and "your payout" is visible at a glance. *(对比表主体由 config 渲染组件输出，本骨架是页面文案与说明区)*

## What each column means
- **Selling / final value fee:** the platform's core percentage — where it applies (item only vs item + shipping) is noted per platform, because that detail alone can flip a comparison.
- **Payment processing:** a separate percentage-plus-fixed line on most platforms; Poshmark is the notable exception above $15.
- **Fixed fees:** per-order add-ons and listing fees that weigh heaviest on small sales.
- **Buyer-side fees:** who pays protection/shipping — it does not change your payout, but it changes what buyers are willing to pay.

## How to read this table honestly
Two platforms can show similar headline rates and pay out very differently once the fee base (does shipping count?) and stacking (is processing separate?) differ. The single-sale calculators linked under the table let you check your exact price instead of trusting averages. *(每行末尾挂对应计算器内链)*

## FAQ
**Which selling app has the lowest fees?**
For sellers: Vinted (no seller commission on standard listings) is the floor; above $15, Poshmark's single-line commission beats stacked structures; eBay/Etsy/Mercari/Depop stack two-plus lines. For the whole picture, compare your actual price point — the spread is printed in the table and each row links to its calculator.

**Do marketplace fees change?**
Yes — structures and rates change, sometimes yearly. Every number on this page renders from our verified config with its verification date shown; stale numbers are marked rather than silently displayed. *(机制句，增强 60 天可信度)*

**Are buyer protection fees my cost?**
No — buyer-side fees come out of the buyer's checkout total, not your payout. But they raise the buyer's total, which can affect conversions; the table flags them per platform.
