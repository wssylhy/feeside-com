## The part most sellers miss: fees on shipping

The label price is only half the story. The other half is **whether your platform's commission is calculated on the shipping too**. On a $30 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping, that difference alone moves your payout:

| Platform | Commission base | Verified fee on a $30 item + $8 shipping |
|---|---|---|
| Mercari | Item price **+ buyer-paid shipping** | {{rates.mercari.final_value_percent}}% × $38 = **$3.80** |
| eBay | Total amount of the sale **including shipping** (most categories) | {{rates.ebay.final_value_percent}}% × $38 + per-order fee |
| Etsy | Item price **+ shipping + gift wrap** | {{rates.etsy.final_value_percent}}% × $38 + payment fee + listing fee |
| Vinted | Seller pays **nothing** — 0% commission | **$0** |
| Depop | 0% selling fee (US); Depop Payments charged on the item price + shipping | payment processing only |

Numbers from each platform's official fee page, cross-checked before publishing — see the calculators below for live math.

## What this means for which platform you pick

- **Heavy or cheap-to-ship items** (small, light, sturdy): Vinted's zero commission keeps the whole payout — shipping is the buyer's problem there.
- **Something heavy on Mercari**: the 20–50 lb tier jumps hard ($35.99–$67.99 best). Price it into the item; and remember Mercari's 10% also takes a cut of whatever shipping you charge.
- **eBay**: the 13.6% eats part of your shipping charge too — on freight-heavy items that line alone can beat the item commission.

## FAQ

**Who pays shipping on Mercari?**
Usually the buyer, at checkout — the seller buys a discounted prepaid label from the chart above and Mercari adds the shipping cost to the buyer's total. You can also offer free shipping and price it into the item, but the 10% selling fee applies to the combined amount either way.

**What is the cheapest Mercari shipping label in 2026?**
For packages under 1 lb, USPS Ground Advantage at the best rate: $5.39–$7.99. Above 2 lb, UPS SurePost takes over as the budget option ($8.49 at 2 lb) until it caps out at 5 lb.

**Does Mercari's selling fee include shipping?**
Yes — the 10% selling fee applies to the item price **plus buyer-paid shipping** on listings created or updated since January 6, 2025.

**Do eBay and Etsy charge fees on shipping too?**
Yes — eBay's final value fee applies to the total amount of the sale including buyer-paid shipping (most categories), and Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee applies to item price plus shipping. Vinted charges sellers nothing.
